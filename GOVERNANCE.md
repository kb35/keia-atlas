# Governance

Keia Atlas is a small open-source project. This page says who decides and how that will change as it grows.

## Today (0.x preview)

- There is one maintainer, Keith Brady ([@kb35](https://github.com/kb35)), who has the final say on the project during the 0.x preview.
- Decisions are made in the open on GitHub: in issues, pull requests and discussions. Design decisions are recorded in [`docs/decisions/`](docs/decisions/). Anyone can propose a change or disagree with one, in public.
- The maintainer listens first and explains the reasons for a decision, especially a "no".

## Contributor ladder

- **Contributor:** anyone who has had a change merged (code, data, docs or a good bug report).
- **Reviewer:** a contributor with several merged changes over at least two months who reviews others' pull requests well and follows the rule book. Reviewers are asked for a review; they cannot merge.
- **Maintainer:** a reviewer with a sustained record (about six months of regular work), who is nominated by a maintainer and agreed by the other maintainers. Maintainers can merge and share in decisions.

## Growing beyond one maintainer

Once two or more organisations contribute regularly, the project will form a small maintainer council, with maintainers drawn from those organisations. Voting will be balanced across organisations so that no one organisation holds a majority: each organisation gets one vote, however many of its people are maintainers. The council decides by open discussion first and a vote only if needed. Until then, the single maintainer decides.

## Changing this page

Changes to governance are proposed as a pull request to this file, discussed in the open for at least 14 days, and made by the maintainer during 0.x. Once a council exists, they need a two-thirds vote of the council.
